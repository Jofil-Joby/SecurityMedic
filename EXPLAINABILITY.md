## Decision and Reasoning

SecurityMedic makes an assessment by analyzing evidence related to source and configuration. It connects detected problems to supporting evidence and practical actions.

## Inputs and Data Sources

SecurityMedic uses source files, configuration, project structure, and relevant source and configuration data from the inspected project.

## Limits and Constraints

SecurityMedic is limited when required information is missing, inaccessible, generated dynamically, or incomplete.
